Why each tag matters

  • charset: shows all letters and symbols correctly.
  • viewport: makes the page fit properly on phones.
  • description: the short text shown under the title in Google search.
  • title: the name on the browser tab and in search results.
  • author: says who made the page.
  • robots: tells search engines if they may show the page.
  • theme-color: colours the browser bar on phones.
  • og:title, og:description: the title and text when the link is shared on social apps.
